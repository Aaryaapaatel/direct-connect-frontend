Got a Question for Direct Connect?

Here are the answers people look for most. Still curious? Sign up from the top right and look around.

What is Direct Connect?

A platform where brands and creators find each other, agree on terms, fund campaigns and release payments milestone by milestone — all in one place.

How are payments protected?

Brands fund the campaign upfront through a regulated payment partner. The money is held for that campaign and released only when a milestone is approved.

How do milestones work?

A campaign is split into steps, such as brief, draft, published content and report. Each step has its own deliverable, amount and approval.

What if something goes wrong?

Either side can raise a dispute with evidence. The other side responds, the platform reviews it, and the funds follow the resolution.

Who can join?

Brands with a verified business and creators with verified identity and social accounts.
